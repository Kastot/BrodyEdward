Null-One

Hacker • Writer • Explorer of the Cosmos • Founder of CodeScribe

I'm an autodidact with a passion for creation, theory, and mathematics. I study advanced mathematics, theoretical physics (To some degree), psychology, ethical hacking, programming, and I have fallen in love with encryption. I am also working on an encryption algorithm called, "Krypto" which is still in development.

Other than my love for studies, I love reading and working on my own theories based in mathematics (Kind of studying, but this I do more for fun, as it is just hypothetical). My active theory (Null-One) was an independent creation of vector mathematics. Prior to understanding or knowing about vectors in linear algebra, I had a theory where a bit or variable could hold more than one piece of data at the same time, specifically the variable and 0, hence why it's called Null-One. I was excited to figure something out even though it was extremely similar to something already known and used in real world mathematics. Though Null-One isn't a breakthrough or even useful, I am very proud to say I thought of something like it.
