How It Works

  1. Send the request form below. The more detail, the better the quote.
  2. I reply within a few days with the scope, price, and timeline in writing.
  3. We agree on the scope. Anything beyond it is quoted separately, so a small project can't quietly grow into a long one.
  4. I deliver the work, you review it, and revisions within the agreed scope are included.

I won't do anything that targets people or systems without authorization. That includes unauthorized access, doxxing, stalking, or harassment, and I'll decline those requests.
